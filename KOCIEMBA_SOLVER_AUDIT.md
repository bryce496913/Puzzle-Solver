# 3×3 two-phase solver isolation audit

This pass audits `Cube3x3KociembaSolver` only. It deliberately does not wire the
solver into `CubeSolvingService`, and the `cube-3x3` catalog entry remains
`comingSoon`.

## Defects found before repair

1. `shouldTry` rejected **both** orders of adjacent commuting opposite faces
   (`U D` and `D U`, and likewise on the other axes). This removed valid
   canonical branches rather than merely deduplicating them. The retained
   convention is now U-before-D, L-before-R, and F-before-B.
2. A caller's `maxNodes` was silently raised to 1,000. The search now treats a
   non-positive value as zero and otherwise observes the exact supplied limit.
3. A caller's zero timeout was silently raised to 0.1 seconds. It is now an
   immediate, typed timeout. Negative timeouts likewise have zero budget.
4. `maxDepth` did not describe the actual search: phase one was silently raised
   to at least seven and total depth to at least twenty. It is now the total
   solution-depth ceiling, with phase one additionally capped at the standard
   twelve-move bound.
5. Search outcomes did not distinguish node exhaustion from depth exhaustion,
   and exposed no phase depths or timing. Direct results now include typed
   termination, phase-one depth, phase-two depth, total length, node count,
   search elapsed time, and pruning-table preparation time.

## Representation and coordinate findings

Sticker storage is six row-major faces in U, R, F, D, L, B order. Conversion
normalizes colors from the six fixed centers, identifies every corner and edge,
derives corner twist and edge flip from ordered facelets, and rejects twist,
flip, duplicate-cubie, and permutation-parity violations. Move transforms are
generated by applying geometric sticker permutations to a solved cube and then
using that same validated conversion.

Phase one uses the 3^7 corner-orientation, 2^11 edge-orientation, and 12-choose-4
slice-combination coordinates. Its goal requires all orientations solved and
the four slice edges in slice positions. Phase two uses full corner permutation,
the eight U/D-edge permutation, and four slice-edge permutation under the
restricted U/D quarter turns plus all half turns. Coordinate table sizes and
index ranges are therefore 2,187, 2,048, 495, 40,320, 40,320, and 24.

Pruning tables are breadth-first distances from solved coordinates. The
coordinates used are closed under their respective move sets, so retaining one
representative state per coordinate is valid. Search lower bounds take the
maximum of component distances and are admissible. Iterative deepening enters
phase two only at the exact phase-one goal and reconstructs the result by
concatenating the current phase-one path with the returned phase-two path.

## Proof strategy

The deterministic regression suite directly invokes the two-phase solver for
solved, shallow, and longer mixed scrambles (including all faces, inverse and
double turns, opposite-face transitions, and axis changes). Every successful
solution is replayed from the known sticker state through the sticker move
engine rather than trusted from the cubie search result. Separate tests cover
the canonical move filter and exact node/timeout behavior. The implementation
has no cancellation API, so cancellation is not claimed or tested in this
isolation pass.
